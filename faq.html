FAQ

How long does ytflex keep my file?

ytflex keeps your file for 1 hour. After 1 hour, ytflex deletes the file. Download or save the file before the hour ends.

What are the limits?

A downloaded file cannot be larger than 2GB. For video downloads, audio is added on top of this limit, so the final file can be a little larger.

ytflex also limits how often you can request a download: 1 request per 10 seconds, and 5 requests per minute, per IP address. If you go over this, ytflex rejects the request with an error. Wait a short time, then try again.

What data does ytflex collect?

ytflex uses your IP address only to enforce the rate limit above. ytflex does not require an account. ytflex does not collect your name or email. ytflex does not link your IP address to a profile.
